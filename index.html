What We Run

From kit to classroom to competition.

KITS

STEM Kits with Design Challenges

Each kit centers on one STEM pathway and includes a QR code linking to weekly, monthly, and yearly challenges — so we can measure real engagement, not just boxes shipped.

COMP

Online Competitions

91 workshops hosted and 267 competition participants so far, across challenges from popsicle-stick bridges to circuit-building speed rounds.

TECH

Computer & Tech Donations

13 laptops donated to date, helping bridge the digital divide for students who need a device to keep learning.

MENT

Workshops & Mentorship

Student-led sessions covering coding in Python, circuits, and robotics basics — built to make complex ideas approachable.

FAIR

Virtual Science Fairs

A space for participants to showcase projects and get real feedback from mentors and judges.

Challenge Library

7 challenges. Every kid picks their lane.

Each challenge ties to a real STEM pathway — students choose the one that fits their kit.

🏗️

Popsicle Stick Bridge Challenge

CIVIL / STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING

Fixed materials, fixed span, judged on max load held before breaking and strength-to-weight efficiency.

📦

Paper Airplane Distance & Accuracy

AERODYNAMICS / PHYSICS

One sheet of paper, three throws, scored on distance and landing accuracy.

🧠

STEM Quiz Bowl

GENERAL STEM KNOWLEDGE

Biology, physics, engineering, and math logic — individual or team-based, in person or online.

🍡

Marshmallow Tower Challenge

STRUCTURAL DESIGN / TEAMWORK

Tallest free-standing tower in 20–30 minutes, judged on height, stability, and design creativity.

⚡

Circuit Building Speed Challenge

ELECTRICAL ENGINEERING

Build a working LED circuit with limited components — fastest correct build wins.

🎨

STEM Innovation Pitch Contest

ENTREPRENEURSHIP / DESIGN

Pitch a STEM-based solution — judged on creativity, feasibility, and impact.

🚀

Balloon Rocket Race

NEWTON'S LAWS / PROPULSION

One balloon, one straight track, scored on fastest time or farthest distance.
